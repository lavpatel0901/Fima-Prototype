# FIMA: High-Fidelity Prototype

**SIT317 Information Technology Innovations and Entrepreneurship, Task 8.2HD**
Lav Patel (s225200046), Team 12

FIMA is a mobile money app for Australian university students with changing income. It shows one clear number, **safe to spend today**, after rent, bills and savings goals are set aside. It also covers expense and income tracking, budgeting, bill reminders, saving goals, an emergency fund, bill splitting with housemates, AI-style spending insights, student tips and privacy controls.

## How to run it

1. Open the live link, or download `index.html` and open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
2. On a laptop, the app appears inside a phone frame, with a guide panel on the left that lists every feature and the test tasks. On a phone, it fills the screen like a real app.
3. Click **Load demo account (Priya)** to see the app with a month of sample data, or **Start fresh** to sign up as a new student.

## Notes

- All data is sample data and is saved in your browser only. Please do not enter real bank details or passwords.
- The bank connection (Consumer Data Right), AI suggestions, push notifications and payments are simulated. Everything else is working, including the safe-to-spend calculation, budgets, goals, bill splitting and privacy controls.
- The final app is planned to be built with Flutter and Firebase.
